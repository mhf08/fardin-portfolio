Taming the Bullwhip

Undergraduate thesis · Dept. of IPE, BUET

Can machine-learning forecasting and blockchain transparency tame the bullwhip effect when geopolitical shocks destabilise lead times?

Defended June 2026 · Supervised by Prof. Dr. Sultana Parveen · with Sheikh Mahir Abrar · Manuscript in preparation

  • 7,218Mean bullwhip ratio at Actor 2 under geopolitical disruption, up from 4,716
  • 5,740Simulation days modelled, trading days 2000–2021
  • 1,000+Simulation runs across six forecasting models

The experiment

  • A five-echelon simulator running a 5,740-day horizon
  • Six forecasting models under rolling walk-forward validation
  • Three visibility modes tested with t-tests and Cohen's d

The findings

  • Disruption dominates forecasting method and information visibility combined
  • The accuracy paradox: the least accurate model suppressed the bullwhip best
  • Pipeline visibility ≈ full visibility, so firms need not share everything
