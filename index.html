Thesis: “Taming the Bullwhip”

Sheet 05 / 09 · Undergraduate Research · Dept. of IPE, BUET

Defended June 2026 · Supervised by Prof. Dr. Sultana Parveen · with Sheikh Mahir Abrar · Manuscript in preparation Taming the Bullwhip Under Geopolitical Uncertainty: A Multi-Echelon Simulation of Machine Learning Forecasting and Blockchain-Enabled Information Visibility.

The Experiment

Can ML forecasting and blockchain transparency tame the bullwhip effect when geopolitical shocks destabilize lead times? To find out, we built:

  • A five-echelon supply chain simulator running a 5,740-day horizon (trading days, 2000–2021)
  • Six forecasting models: Traditional, History, ARIMA, XGBoost, LightGBM, and Temporal Fusion Transformer, all under rolling walk-forward validation
  • Two disruption scenarios: gamma-distributed lead times, modulated daily by the Geopolitical Risk Index
  • Three blockchain visibility modes: none, pipeline, and full, run across four configurations and seven seeds and tested with t-tests and Cohen's d

The Findings

  • Disruption dominates: geopolitical shocks amplified the bullwhip beyond the combined effects of forecasting method and information visibility; the mean bullwhip ratio at Actor 2 rose from 4,716 to 7,218.
  • The accuracy–performance paradox: TFT had the poorest point-forecast accuracy among ML models yet the strongest bullwhip suppression, while XGBoost, the most accurate, performed no better than a naive rolling average.
  • ARIMA broke down structurally under disruption, turning erratic with physically implausible results.
  • Pipeline visibility ≈ full visibility: any transparency reduced network inventory, but pipeline-only sharing was statistically equivalent to full transparency.

What It Means for Practice

P-01 Build resilience first: inventory buffers and supplier diversification outweigh forecasting upgrades or information systems when disruption looms.

P-02 When choosing a forecasting model, weight the consistency of its replenishment signals more heavily than its historical point accuracy.

P-03 For blockchain, pipeline visibility captures the primary benefit without the governance burden of full transparency.

CASE 06-A · Industrial Safety for Fertilizer Manufacturing

Smart IoT-Based Ammonia Gas Monitoring System

  • IoT
  • Embedded Sensors
  • Industrial Safety
  • Web Dashboard

Overview

In fertilizer plants, Ammonia is a volatile necessity. I co-developed a low-cost, real-time monitoring system to automate safety responses where manual oversight often fails.

Stack / Engineering

MQ-137 (Ammonia) via ADS1115 (16-bit ADC) and DHT22 (Temp/Humidity), ESP-01S (Wi-Fi) streaming to web dashboard, threshold-based logic (30 ppm Ammonia / 30°C) triggering Buzzer & Fan.

Impact

Reduced response time to leaks through automated targeted actuation. Created a centralized, auditable data stream. Modular scalability for rapid retrofitting.

CASE 06-B · Pedal Powered Water Pump for Rural Sustainability

The HydroPedal

  • SolidWorks
  • Ansys FEA
  • CFD
  • Sustainable Design

Overview

In many rural agricultural regions, small-scale farmers face a “fuel-cost trap.” We engineered the HydroPedal to decouple irrigation from fossil fuels.

Stack / Engineering

Complete 3D assembly in SolidWorks, Ansys Static Structural analysis on frame, CFD for impeller housing optimization, Material selection: AISI 1020 Steel.

Impact

Flow rate 25–30 L/min at 20 ft head. 100% reduction in operational fuel costs. Sustainable alternative for independent farmers.
