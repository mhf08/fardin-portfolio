Projects

Selected engineering work

Industrial Safety for Fertilizer Manufacturing

Smart IoT-Based Ammonia Gas Monitoring System

30 ppmAmmonia level at which the fan and alarm switch on automatically

  • IoT
  • Embedded Sensors
  • Industrial Safety
  • Web Dashboard

Overview

In fertilizer plants, Ammonia is a volatile necessity. I co-developed a low-cost, real-time monitoring system to automate safety responses where manual oversight often fails.

Stack / Engineering

MQ-137 (Ammonia) via ADS1115 (16-bit ADC) and DHT22 (Temp/Humidity), ESP-01S (Wi-Fi) streaming to web dashboard, threshold-based logic (30 ppm Ammonia / 30°C) triggering Buzzer & Fan.

Impact

Actuation is automatic, so nothing waits on someone noticing. Every reading is logged, which turns a spot check into a record. Modular enough to retrofit onto a line already running.

Pedal Powered Water Pump for Rural Sustainability

The HydroPedal

25–30 L/minat 20 ft of head, with no fuel and no electricity

  • SolidWorks
  • Ansys FEA
  • CFD
  • Sustainable Design

Overview

In many rural agricultural regions, small-scale farmers face a “fuel-cost trap.” We engineered the HydroPedal to decouple irrigation from fossil fuels.

Stack / Engineering

Complete 3D assembly in SolidWorks, chain-sprocket drive modelled and gear ratio tuned for local manufacturability, Ansys Static Structural analysis on frame, CFD for impeller housing optimization, Material selection: AISI 1020 Steel.

Impact

25–30 L/min at 20 ft of head, on no fuel and no electricity. Built from parts a local workshop can source and repair.

Radial Drilling Machine SolidWorks Model

50+components, with full arm travel and spindle rotation and no interference

  • SolidWorks
