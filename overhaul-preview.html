Doctoral-track work · Undergraduate thesis

Taming the Bullwhip Under Geopolitical Uncertainty

Defended June 2026 · Supervised by Prof. Dr. Sultana Parveen · with Sheikh Mahir Abrar

  • 7,218Mean bullwhip ratio at Actor 2 under geopolitical disruption, up from 4,716
  • 5,740Simulation days modelled, trading days 2000–2021
  • 1,000+Simulation runs across six forecasting models

Findings

  • Disruption dominates forecasting method and information visibility combined.
  • The accuracy paradox: the least accurate ML model suppressed the bullwhip best, while the most accurate performed no better than a naive rolling average.
  • ARIMA broke down structurally, a failure mode I have not found documented in the literature.
  • Pipeline visibility ≈ full visibility, so firms need not share everything to get the benefit.
